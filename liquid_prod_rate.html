Liquifier Production Rate

This page shows fill rate of the mother dewar (MD) in the periods the level is increasing: likely no one is drawing liquid from the MD during these times. In this case the fill rate of the MD will be the liquifier production rate.
  • MD Level in percent corresponds to the readback from LVL204
  • The MD fill rate is the slope of a linear fit to the region where the MD level is increasing. See the colored regions for fitted points.
  • The MD surplus fill rate is the difference between the minimum points of the linear fits divided by the duration between each minima.
  • The average return flow is the FM210 readings averaged over the region where the MD level is increasing. The error bars are the standard deviation within that time frame.
This assumes that the conversion from % to L in the MD is 12.6 L/%
